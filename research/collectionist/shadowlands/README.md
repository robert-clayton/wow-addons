# Collectionist Shadowlands release inventory

This directory records the frozen ID and acquisition audit used by Collectionist's **Shadowlands** (`shadowlands`) runtime catalogs.

## Snapshot boundary

- Final Battle for Azeroth baseline: `8.3.7.35662`
- Final Shadowlands snapshot: `9.2.7.45745`
- Current-retail existence check: `12.1.0.69382`

DB2 CSVs come from `https://wago.tools/db2/<Table>/csv?build=<build>&locale=enUS`. The generator expects the BFA and Shadowlands snapshots under `%TEMP%/collectionist-sl-db2/` and the current snapshot under `%TEMP%/collectionist-df-db2/current/`.

Run:

```powershell
./scripts/generate-collectionist-shadowlands-id-inventory.ps1
```

## Expansion ownership rule

Housing decorations belong to the expansion that supplies their acquisition path. Midnight's housing launch, a current DB2 row, an item expansion field, and a visual/theme tag do not assign ownership.

The live Shadowlands theme filter currently returns 33 rows: `https://housing.wowdb.com/decor/?tags=shadowlands`. The acquisition audit resolves them as:

- 25 Shadowlands-owned decorations: 23 Shadowlands profession crafts, Portal to Damnation, and Venthyr Tome of Unforgiven Sins.
- 1 Dragonflight-owned decoration: Bel'ameth Traveler's Pack.
- 1 The War Within-owned decoration: Cartel Collector's Cage.
- 1 Midnight-owned decoration: Stretched Snakeskin Rack.
- 3 hidden rows with no acquisition source.
- 2 internal autogenerated DNT rows.

A full-catalog acquisition scan also found Stormwind Wooden Table outside the Shadowlands theme filter. Its unlock is quest 59583, `Welcome to Stormwind`, from the Exile's Reach new-player path introduced with Shadowlands.

`sources/housing-wowdb-acquisition-audit.csv` keeps the theme tag and acquisition expansion separate. Only the 26 `acquisition_shadowlands_confirmed` rows are eligible for the Shadowlands release manifest.

## Current ID totals

- Mount snapshot candidates: 193; 179 selected, 12 external-policy exclusions, and 2 internal/test exclusions.
- Battle-pet snapshot candidates: 300; 175 selected, 6 external-policy exclusions, and 119 noncollectible or unavailable species rows.
- Toy snapshot candidates: 121; 115 selected, 4 external-policy exclusions, and 2 unavailable/internal exclusions. Toy 1164/item 187959 is a removed placeholder and is absent from current retail.
- Decorations: 33 themed candidates plus 1 full-catalog acquisition match; 26 acquisition-confirmed for Shadowlands.
- Achievement snapshot candidates: 963.
- Player-visible Shadowlands achievements: 419 across the seven player-facing categories.
- Ordered achievement criteria: 2,489.
- Profession recipe abilities: 636, comprising 611 named historical recipes, 2 unnamed historical DB2 candidates, and 23 current housing recipes taught by Shadowlands profession tiers.
- Rare criteria candidates: 240; 211 selected after excluding the 29 criteria duplicated by partial achievement 15512.
- Treasure criteria candidates: 130; 103 selected after excluding the 27 criteria duplicated by partial achievement 15513.
- Broad support candidates: 326 maps, 2 expansion-flagged faction headers, and 149 currency deltas. Exact dependencies are frozen to 8 primary maps, 12 reputations/friendships, and 9 currencies; every selected support ID still exists in current retail.

The twelve exact manifests are under `manifests/`. They freeze IDs for mounts, pets, toys, decorations, achievements and criteria, recipes, rares, treasures, maps, factions, and currencies.

Every selected mount, pet, and toy now has nonblank acquisition text. Nineteen toys whose final-Shadowlands DB2 source text was blank are overlaid from their documented covenant, profession, Zereth Mortis, and Winter Veil acquisition paths; the overlay is ID-anchored so future tooltip changes cannot alter membership.

Availability is also ID-anchored. Ten mounts are legacy: the four Gladiator Soul Eaters, four seasonal Deathwalkers, Carcinized Zerethsteed, and Jigglesworth Sr. Putrid Geist is legacy because Death Rising ended with the Shadowlands launch. The selected Winter Veil gifts, Doomwalker Trophy Stand, and Shadowlands vicious PvP mounts remain obtainable through recurring events or Vicious Saddles and are not marked unavailable.

## Rare and treasure scope

The selected rare groups use achievements `14307`, `14308`, `14309`, `14310`, `14660`, `14744`, `15054`, `15107`, `15391`, and `15392`. Achievement `15512` repeats all 29 Zereth Mortis encounter IDs from `15391` and is excluded as a partial duplicate.

`Adventurer of Bastion` is the one rare group whose 29 criteria expose completion quest IDs rather than creature IDs. `sources/bastion-rare-npc-audit.csv` resolves every position to its encounter NPC, including the combat forms for the Beasts of Bastion, Nikara Blackheart and Selena the Reborn for the two Vesper Repair events, Larionrider Orstus as the representative for the linked-health duo, and the Ascended Council wrapper. The generated rare inventory and manifest carry `npc_ids` for all 211 selected criteria, and generation fails if any mapping is blank.

The selected treasure groups use achievements `14311`, `14312`, `14313`, `14314`, `15099`, `15331`, and `15502`. Achievement `15513` repeats all 27 Zereth Mortis treasure labels from `15331` and is excluded as a partial duplicate.

## Recipe scope

Recipe IDs are collected by traversing the child categories of the nine final-Shadowlands profession headers: Alchemy `1293`, Blacksmithing `1310`, Cooking `1322`, Leatherworking `1333`, Enchanting `1363`, Engineering `1380`, Tailoring `1394`, Inscription `1405`, and Jewelcrafting `1417`.

## Runtime and validation

`scripts/generate-collectionist-shadowlands-lua.ps1` renders the exact manifests into the eight Shadowlands runtime files. `scripts/validate-collectionist-shadowlands-runtime.ps1` verifies exact collectible and recipe ID sets, ordered rare/treasure criteria, all 211 rare NPC positions, availability flags, achievement task criteria, and TOC coverage. The mocked WoW runtime tests also load Shadowlands alongside every later expansion and reject cross-expansion ID collisions.

Run the release checks with:

```powershell
./scripts/validate-collectionist-shadowlands-manifests.ps1
./scripts/validate-collectionist-shadowlands-runtime.ps1
luajit ./tests/run.lua
luajit ./tests/toc_smoke.lua
```
